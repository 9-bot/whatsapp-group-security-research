WhatsApp Group Security Research (Ivan Schroll)

Open access papers by Ivan Schroll (ORCID https://orcid.org/0009-0007-0680-5570) on the security, privacy and governance of WhatsApp groups. Each paper is a narrative review released under CC BY 4.0 on OSF.

  • Account Takeover of WhatsApp Group Administrators: Verification-Code Hijacking, Linked Devices and Layered Mitigations
    DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/HZUN5
  • Scams and Malicious Links in WhatsApp Groups: Employment, Investment and Impersonation Fraud, Pix Payments in Brazil and Administrator-Level Controls
    DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/CYP2G
  • Access Control and Member Privacy in Large WhatsApp Groups: Invite-Link Exposure, Phone-Number Disclosure and Unwanted Entry
    DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/WFDQ3
  • Personal Data Protection in Business-Operated WhatsApp Groups: Controller Duties, Lawful Basis, Phone-Number Exposure, Retention and Incident Response under the LGPD and the GDPR
    DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/ZG9U7
  • Governance and Operational Security of Business WhatsApp Group Portfolios: Administrator Lifecycle, Employee Offboarding, Brand Impersonation and Recordkeeping
    DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/G6HF9

The author is the founder of 9bot, a WhatsApp group management tool. 9bot is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta or WhatsApp.
